## 2. Domain Review Findings & Verification

### 2.1 Network & Transport Layer (Layer 4 Post-TCP-Stack Placement)
- **Observation**: Because the IPS extractor sits at **Layer 4 (after the OS TCP stack)**, lower-layer TCP retransmissions and out-of-order TCP segments are absorbed and ordered by the kernel TCP stack before reaching [`IngressIps`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/ingress_ips.rs) or [`EgressIps`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/egress_ips.rs).
- **Simplicity & Surgical Alignment (Karpathy Rule 2)**: No speculative TCP retransmission deduplication cache (`absorb_network_retransmissions` / `recent_verified_pdus`) is included in [`VerifierConfig`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/egress_ips.rs#L38-L51) or [`EgressIps`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/egress_ips.rs#L117-L136), keeping the state machine minimal and ensuring genuine application-layer PDU replays are always caught as `IntegrityAlert::PduAdded`.
- **TCP Stream Coalescing / Splitting**: At Layer 4, reading from a TCP stream buffer can coalesce multiple PDUs into a single read or split them across reads. Because [`compute_pdu_xyi_mac`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/crypto.rs#L172-L184) and the sliding-window reconciler operate at the individual `Pdu` granularity, TCP stream boundary shifts produce **zero false positives** (verified in [`test_nominal_multi_pdu_and_non_critical_mutation_and_tcp_coalescing`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/tests/integration_tests.rs#L80-L157)).

### 2.2 Zero-False-Positive Guarantees (Remote Egress & Local Storage PCAP)
1. **Global & Per-Flow Sensor-to-IPS Clock Offset Normalization**:
   - [`EgressIps`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/egress_ips.rs#L128-L131) tracks `global_clock_offset_us` in addition to per-flow `sensor_to_ips_clock_offset_us` and normalizes `first_seen` in [`evaluate_single_flow`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/egress_ips.rs#L412-L433). When a brand-new flow from a lagging sensor clock has its data packet arrive slightly before its first Control Frame, it is normalized into the IPS wall-clock domain and never triggers a false `FlowAdded`.
2. **Two-Sided Window Guard on Live `.pcap` Verification (`[min_retained_ts_us, max_retained_ts_us]`)**:
   - [`verify_packets_with_frames`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/pcap_verifier.rs#L417-L495) clips both pre-rotation packets (`ts < min_retained_ts_us` when `first_frame.frame_seq > 0`) and in-flight tail packets (`ts > max_retained_ts_us` whose control frame has not yet been flushed to disk) on known flows, while evaluating final watermarks bounded by `max_observed_pcap_ts + control_skew_tolerance_us` so trailing control frames never trigger false `PduDeleted`.
3. **Chronological Multi-Session Ordering in `RotatingControlStore`**:
   - [`read_all_frames`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/storage.rs#L221-L241) and [`read_all_frames_live`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-TrustChain/src/storage.rs#L254-L283) preserve segment chronological order across stateless `IngressIps` reboots (`boot_session_id` transitions) and deduplicate strictly by `(sender_id, boot_session_id, frame_seq)`.
